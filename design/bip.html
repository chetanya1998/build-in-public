Two mascots. Pick your buddy.

Bip is a squishy jelly blob with bunny ears and little feet, drawn as a soft 3D toy that can move every part: ears, eyes, feet, squash and stretch.

Sarthak's buddy is a charcoal felt block with a screen for a face, rendered in seven poses: notebook, shipped, excited, thinking, detective, grumpy and sleepy.

Both keep the same promise: they never post for you. You choose which one keeps you company.

Sarthak's seven poses

The source renders live in Mascot/. These web copies (in design/mascot/) are 640px WebP files, about 30 KB each. Five of the originals had a checkerboard painted into the pixels, which was removed for these copies; transparent exports from Sarthak would be cleaner.

Bip's colours

Five bodies. The scarf always takes the colour of the project Bip is following. Charcoal Bip borrows Sarthak's big white eyes.

Bip's accessories

Each one tells you something: which project, deep work, a celebration, bedtime, your streak.

Sizes

Both mascots are smooth at any size, from a 24px avatar to a full hero.

Rules for both

Never sad about youNo wilting, no crying, no guilt when you skip a day. Grumpy is only ever aimed at a bug.
Never in the waySits above windows, dodges a rushing cursor, steps out of screenshots.
Never loudNo sounds, slow small motion, one bubble every 45 minutes at most.
Never a stand-in for youDrafts nothing on its own and posts nothing at all.
Readable at a glanceBip's ears and the buddy's pose each say the mood from across the screen.
