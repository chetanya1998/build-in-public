# Walkthrough: two-step onboarding

Onboarding went from 5 steps to 2. Step 1 asks for your name and business details (tax settings now have sensible defaults by country). Step 2 creates your first invoice from a sample client, so the first thing a new user sees is a finished invoice instead of a settings form.

The logo upload moved to Settings and is optional.
